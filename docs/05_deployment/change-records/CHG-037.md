## Summary

The compute launcher no longer describes or loops over the retired `feature-compute` and
`signal-strategy` jobs. It uploads the compute jar once, submits the implemented Signal and
Babysitter entry points through the Flink REST API, and fails closed unless each submitted job
reaches `RUNNING`. The Compose comment now reflects the same two-job topology.

Feature computation remains inside `SignalJob`; no separate feature-compute deployment is added.

- The `/jars/{jar_id}/run` probe resolved the jar and rejected only the intentionally bogus entry class.
- The uploaded probe jar was deleted successfully; no dev job was submitted by the probe.
