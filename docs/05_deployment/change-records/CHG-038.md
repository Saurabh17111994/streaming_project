## Summary

DEC-043 removes the fixed seven-year audit duration. The current baseline is **at least one year**,
with longer retention when required by the approved jurisdictional, contractual, legal-hold, or
operational policy.

The change does not remove audit obligations. Money-moving and safety evidence remains immutable,
encrypted, integrity-verifiable, access-controlled, offloaded, recoverable, and governed by
authorized retention-policy changes and two-person deletion evidence.

Historical records such as CHG-036 and the superseded DEC-020 wording remain unchanged for
traceability.
